Bulk and special orders

Ordering many pieces? Send us your design.

How it works

  1. 1Upload your artwork and tell us the product, colour, sizes and quantities
  2. 2We check the file and reply by email with a total and a date
  3. 3You confirm, we press it by hand in Tallinn and deliver, or you collect it by agreement

There is no minimum order. For bulk orders the price and timing depend on the size of the order, so we quote each one. Delivery is free in Estonia and on orders over €100.

For a single piece, use the customizer and add it to your cart.

We only print artwork you own or have permission to use. We cannot print protected characters, logos or brand names.
