Shipping

Where we deliver, how long it takes and what it costs.

Delivery prices

CountryPrice
Estonia (launch offer)Free
Latvia, Lithuania€5
Finland, Sweden, Germany and other EU countries€7

Delivery is free on every order over €100. For delivery outside the EU, please ask us first.

Local pickup in Tallinn

You can collect your order in Tallinn, by agreement with the Specra team. Email hello@specra.store to arrange a time. We work flexible hours, so there are no fixed opening hours.

How long it takes

A single order is ready to dispatch in about 7 days. After that, parcels usually take 1 to 3 business days within Estonia and about 3 to 7 business days to other EU countries. Bulk orders depend on the size of the order: send us the details and we confirm a date.

Delivery problems

Please check your parcel when it arrives and tell us at once if it is damaged or missing. Write to hello@specra.store with your order number.

Correct address

Please check your address and phone number when you order. If a parcel comes back because of a wrong address, we will contact you about sending it again.
