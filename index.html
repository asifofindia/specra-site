Good to know

Questions, answered.

All questions
How long does an order take?

A single order is ready to dispatch in about 7 days. For bulk orders the time depends on the size of the order: send us the details and we give you a date.

How much is delivery?

Free in Estonia during our launch offer. To Latvia and Lithuania it is €5, and to other EU countries €7. Delivery is free on orders over €100. Local pickup in Tallinn is possible by agreement with our team: just email us.

Is there a minimum order?

No. You can order a single piece.

How are the prints made?

We use premium DTF (direct to film) transfers, pressed by hand in Tallinn. They are full colour, with fine detail and smooth gradients, on light and dark garments. The maximum print size is A3 (29.7 × 42 cm).

What are the garments like?

We use premium quality material, chosen for a soft feel and a clean print surface. Use the size guide to choose your size.

How should I wash my print?

Wait 24 hours before the first wash. Turn the garment inside out and wash it at 30°C or colder on a gentle cycle, with a mild detergent. Never iron directly on the print. See the full care guide.
