Privacy policy

What we collect, why, and your rights.

Who is responsible

The controller is Specra OÜ, registry code 17562311, VAT no. EE103014472, Madara 31, shop 22, 10613 Tallinn, Estonia, email hello@specra.store.

What we collect

We collect only what you give us: your name, email, message and topic when you use the contact form; your email when you join the list; your details when you withdraw from an order; artwork and text you upload on the Customize page; and your name, address, phone number and order details when you place an order. Our host keeps standard server logs, such as IP address and time of visit, for security.

Your cart

Your cart, and any artwork you add to it, is kept on your own device (in your browser storage) until you place the order or clear it. We do not use it to track you.

Why and on what basis

  • To answer your messages: our legitimate interest, or steps you asked for before a contract.
  • To send emails about Specra and new designs, and your welcome discount code: your consent. You can withdraw it at any time.
  • To deliver and refund your orders: the contract.
  • To keep accounts and invoices: a legal obligation. Estonian accounting law requires us to keep them for 7 years.

Who we share it with

We use service providers who process data for us: Netlify (website hosting and form storage), Stripe (card payments), Google (Gmail for our mailbox, and fonts loaded from Google servers), ImprovMX (email forwarding), and parcel carriers. Some are outside the EU. In that case transfers rely on an EU adequacy decision or standard contractual clauses. We do not sell your data.

How long we keep it

Messages: up to 12 months after the conversation ends. Newsletter: until you unsubscribe. Order and accounting records: 7 years.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not set cookies for tracking or advertising. When we switch on visit statistics, we will use a cookie-free tool (Plausible Analytics) and update this page.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, delete, restrict or move your data, and object to how we use it. Write to hello@specra.store. You can also complain to the Estonian Data Protection Inspectorate (Andmekaitse Inspektsioon), aki.ee, info@aki.ee.
