FAQ

Quick answers about orders, delivery and printing.

How long does an order take?

A single order is ready to dispatch in about 7 days. For bulk orders the time depends on the size of the order: send us the details and we give you a date.

How much is delivery?

Free in Estonia during our launch offer. To Latvia and Lithuania it is €5, and to other EU countries €7. Delivery is free on orders over €100. Local pickup in Tallinn is possible by agreement with our team: just email us.

Is there a minimum order?

No. You can order a single piece.

How are the prints made?

We use premium DTF (direct to film) transfers, pressed by hand in Tallinn. They are full colour, with fine detail and smooth gradients, on light and dark garments. The maximum print size is A3 (29.7 × 42 cm).

What are the garments like?

We use premium quality material, chosen for a soft feel and a clean print surface. Use the size guide to choose your size.

How should I wash my print?

Wait 24 hours before the first wash. Turn the garment inside out and wash it at 30°C or colder on a gentle cycle, with a mild detergent. Never iron directly on the print. See the full care guide.

Can I design my own piece?

Yes. Open Customize, choose a product and colour, upload your artwork or add text, place it where you want it and add it to your cart. We check your artwork before we print.

Do you have a discount code?

Yes. Join the list at the bottom of any page and you get a code for 5% off your first order. Enter it in the promotion code box on the secure payment page.

How do I pay?

Add your items to the cart and place your order. We confirm it by email, and you can pay by card through a secure Stripe payment link, or right away from the thank-you page. We never see or store your card details.

Do you deliver outside Estonia?

Yes, to other EU countries. See the delivery prices above, or the Shipping page.

Can I return an order?

Yes. You have 14 days from receiving your order to withdraw, without giving a reason. Custom orders made to your specification are the exception. See Returns and withdrawal.

Can you print a character, band, film or brand logo?

No. We only print artwork you own or have permission to use. We cannot print protected characters, logos or brand names.

Are prices shown with VAT, and can I get an invoice?

Prices include VAT. For a company invoice, email hello@specra.store with your order number and company details.

Something arrived faulty. What now?

Email us with your order number and photos. Your legal rights, including the two-year legal guarantee, always apply. See Returns and withdrawal.

Can I visit the workshop?

Yes, by agreement. We work flexible hours, so please email us first to arrange a time.
