How it works

Three steps, no fuss.

  1. 1Design it. Open the customizer, pick a product and colour, upload your artwork or add text, and place it where you want it.
  2. 2We check it. We look at your artwork before we print, and tell you if anything needs fixing.
  3. 3We press it. Your piece is pressed by hand in Tallinn and ready to dispatch in about 7 days.
